Lyrics, word by word

WordPulse lights up the lyrics of whatever you play, one word at a time, right on your desktop behind your icons.

  • 1
    Just press playSpotify, Apple Music, YouTube Music, Tidal or a browser. No login needed.
  • 2
    It lives in the trayClick the tray icon (by the clock) for settings: 11 styles, colours, position, mood and more.
  • 3
    Shortcuts that work anywhereCtrl+Alt+L hide or show · Ctrl+Alt+S next style · Ctrl+Alt+←/→ nudge timing
  • 4
    Words a bit off?Tray › Sync Words for This Song lets you tap along for perfect timing. It's remembered.
